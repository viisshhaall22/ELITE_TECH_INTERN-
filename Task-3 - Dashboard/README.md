# Task 3: Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
This project involves building an interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard (developed in Tableau) to analyze sales performance across product sub-categories and observe monthly revenue trends over time.

---

## Dashboard Preview

![Dashboard Preview](DASHBOARD%20IMAGE.png)

---

## Visualizations & Key Metrics

* **Total Sales Volume:** ~$733,215 across all product categories.
* **Lollipop Chart (Sales by Sub-Category):** Ranks product categories by total sales. Highlights top performers like **Chairs** ($95,554), **Binders** ($72,788), and **Storage** ($62,899) alongside lower-volume items like Fasteners and Envelopes.
* **Pie Chart (Sales Distribution):** Illustrates the proportional revenue contribution of each sub-category relative to the entire business catalog.
* **Area Chart (Monthly Sales Trend):** Tracks month-over-month sales performance across the year, capturing seasonal spikes peaking in **November** ($118,448) and **September** ($87,867).

---

## Key Insights

* **Top Revenue Drivers:** The highest-selling sub-categories are Chairs, Binders, and Storage, which together account for a substantial share of total revenue.
* **Seasonality Trends:** Sales experience significant upward momentum in the second half of the year (Q3 and Q4), reaching an annual peak in November.
* **Low-Performing Segments:** Fasteners, Envelopes, and Art represent the lowest revenue contributions, indicating potential areas for catalog optimization or targeted promotions.

---

## Tools Used

* **BI Platform:** Tableau / Tableau Public
* **Data Fields:** Order Date, Sales, Sub-Category, Product Hierarchy
